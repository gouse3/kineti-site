Kineti

An ACL rehabilitation companion for iPhone — your programme, your pain, your range of motion, in one place.

What it does

  • Phase-based exercises with hold and rest timers, and an animated demonstration of each movement.
  • A pain log that shows the trend, not just today's number.
  • Attendance tracking, because consistency is the thing that actually moves recovery.
  • Knee angle measurement from a photo you take — freeze a frame, place three points, read the angle.
  • A PDF progress report you can hand to your physiotherapist.
  • Custom exercises, for whatever your physio prescribes that isn't in the library.

Your data stays on your iPhone

Exercises, pain entries, measurements and photos are stored on your device and are never uploaded. Your account holds only your name and email address. There is no analytics and no advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

Kineti Pro

Angle measurement, PDF progress reports and custom exercises are part of Kineti Pro, an auto-renewing subscription available monthly or annually through the App Store. Everything else — the exercise library, the timers, the pain log and attendance — is free.

Kineti is a rehabilitation companion, not a medical device. Always follow your surgeon and physiotherapist.
