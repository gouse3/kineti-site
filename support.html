Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Kineti. Deleting your Kineti account does not cancel the subscription — cancel it there first.

I paid, but Pro isn't unlocked

Open Kineti ▸ Settings ▸ Restore purchases. A subscription belongs to the Apple Account that bought it, so make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same one. If two people share a phone, restoring moves Pro to whichever Kineti account restores it.

Can I get my data back after deleting the app?

No. Everything is stored on your device and nothing is uploaded, which also means there is no copy for us to restore. Export a PDF progress report before you delete anything you care about.

How accurate is the angle measurement?

It is a geometric estimate from three points you place on a photograph. It's good for tracking change week to week; it is not a clinical goniometer reading. Take the photo from the same angle each time and you'll get a more useful trend.

How do I delete my account?

Kineti ▸ Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. It permanently removes your account and every piece of rehab data stored for it on the device. It cannot be undone.
