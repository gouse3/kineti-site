What Kineti is

Kineti helps you follow an ACL rehabilitation programme: it shows exercises with timers, records pain and attendance, and measures knee range of motion from a photograph you take.

What Kineti is not

Kineti is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Nothing in the app replaces your surgeon, physiotherapist or any other qualified clinician.

Exercises shown are general rehabilitation education. Protocols vary by surgeon, graft type and individual circumstances. Follow the programme your clinician gives you. If an exercise causes sharp pain, new swelling or instability, stop and contact them.

Range-of-motion measurements are geometric estimates based on three points you place on a photograph. Accuracy depends on where you place them, your camera angle and your posture. They are useful for tracking change over time, not for clinical decisions.
