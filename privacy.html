Kineti Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Kineti is a companion for ACL rehabilitation. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, what it does not, and what you can do about it.

The short version

Your rehab data stays on your iPhone. Your account stores only your name and email. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising, and we do not share your information with anyone except the service that runs sign-in.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage, and is never uploaded to us:

  • Exercises you complete, including sets, reps and duration
  • Pain entries: level, location, notes and dates
  • Range-of-motion measurements, including any photos taken to measure them
  • Attendance history
  • Custom exercises you or your physiotherapist add
  • Your surgery date, rehab phase and goals

Each account on a device has a separate store, so if more than one person uses the same iPhone, neither can see the other's information.

Because this data is on your device only, we cannot recover it for you. If you delete the app, reset your iPhone or delete your account, it is gone.

What leaves your device

Your name and email address, to create and sign in to your account. These are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores them on Google's servers. Firebase also processes your password; we never see it. Firebase's privacy information is at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Nothing else. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.

Why we need an account

To confirm you own the email address you signed up with, and to keep one person's rehab data separate from another's on a shared device.

Payments

Kineti Pro is sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card details. We only learn that a subscription is active on the device.

Your choices

  • See or change your name: Settings ▸ Profile, at any time.
  • Delete everything: Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all rehab data stored for it on the device. It does not cancel an App Store subscription — do that in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions.
  • Export: Pro subscribers can produce a PDF progress report at any time and share it wherever they like.

Children

Kineti is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Health data

Kineti does not read from or write to Apple Health. The rehab information you enter is health-related and is treated as described above: kept on your device, never uploaded.

Medical disclaimer

Kineti is a rehabilitation companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Range-of-motion measurements are estimates taken from a photograph and are not a clinical assessment. Always follow the guidance of your surgeon and physiotherapist.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will be updated and the date above will change.
